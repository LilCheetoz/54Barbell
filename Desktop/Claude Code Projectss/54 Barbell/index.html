Goal: Get Stronger

"Not a commercial gym. Not a franchise. 54 Barbell is a private training space built for people who take their strength seriously."

Founded by Nathan in Smithers, BC, 54 Barbell takes its name from the 54th parallel — the latitude that runs through our northern home. We built this facility because northern BC deserved better than the average gym experience.

Every piece of equipment was chosen deliberately. Every member was chosen the same way. We keep numbers small so that training here always feels as it should — focused, intentional, and distraction-free.
